# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

This repository contains an individual policy proposal on how the Australian Government can support clerical and administrative workers through the transition to generative AI.

## Files

- `proposal.md` — 1,200-word policy proposal
- `reflection.md` — 200-word reflection on the use of AI

## Policy audience

Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR)

## Working title

*Supporting Clerical and Administrative Workers Through Australia's AI Transition*
